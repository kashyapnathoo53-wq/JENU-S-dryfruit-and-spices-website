Kashmiri Pandit Heritage

Custodians of Purity & Mountain Terroir

Wearing the regal velvet Pheran hand-embroidered with intricate golden Kashmiri Tilla needlework and sacred Dejhoor golden ear ornaments, Kashmiri Pandit women have for centuries preserved the sacred art of grading and curing valley harvests. From dawn hand-plucking of crimson saffron stigmas in Pampore to the meticulous sun-sorting of oil-rich Mamra almonds and paper-shell Kagzi walnuts, purity is revered as devotion.

Waters of Srinagar

The Shikaras & Whispering Lake Waters

Gliding silently across the glassy, mirror-like waters of Dal Lake in Srinagar, ornate wooden Shikaras flanked by cedar houseboats, floating flower markets, and golden autumn Chinar trees form the beating heart of Kashmir. For over five hundred years, these waters carried royal consignments across Himalayan trade routes. Today, JENU'S packages these harvests at the water's edge and dispatches daily air consignments across India.
